**Run a cozy little aquarium shop and breed guppies to build it up.**

Fish Breeder Job Simulator is a relaxed shop game about breeding, decorating and selling guppies. Nothing dies, nothing is rushed, and there's no failing. It's just you, your tanks and a steady stream of customers.

**Breed rare guppies**
- Pair guppies in up to **4 breeding tanks** and watch the fry grow up.
- Mix varieties to find **15 breeding recipes**: Purple, Pink, Gold and more.
- Each new combination you find is recorded in your **breeding book**.
- Rare **mutations** like Albino, Glass, Galaxy and Dragon appear by chance.
- Better parents give better babies. Breed your way from ★ to ★★★★★ stock.

**Run the shop**
- Open up, set out your best fish in the display tank, and serve customers at the counter.
- The better your fish and display look, the more often customers come in.
- Customers ask for particular varieties. Order them in from the Market, or breed one to order and they'll come back for it.
- Take on **special orders** and build a whole decorated tank in the Orders room.
- Sell spare fish wholesale when the tanks get crowded.

**Decorate**
- Plants, rocks, driftwood, sand and ornaments, including an animated bubbling treasure chest.
- A tidy, well-decorated tank sells for more.

**Play at your own pace**
- Stay open as long as you like after the first four hours.
- Close up to restock and speed up time 4× while your fry grow.

*In active development. New varieties, decorations and features are added often. Feedback is very welcome!*
